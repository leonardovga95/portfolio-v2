Projects

I’ve been working with Caioba since November 2025. We’ve been consistently producing weekly videos from a wide variety of games and content, adapting his long-form streams into short-form videos for social media.

I’m currently working with Choco, who is growing his community as a streamer. At the moment, we’re creating content around an EA FC tournament featuring amateur teams.

Vovo has been one of the biggest GTA RP streamers in Brazil since 2019. I worked with him for four months, creating short-form content from his livestreams.

Kiko is the CEO of one of the biggest GTA RP servers in Brazil. I worked with him on around 40 videos. Many of them involved connecting storylines from three or more separate livestreams and turning them into cohesive short-form stories.

My first professional experience as a video editor was with GuiBike. I planned the entire concept, filmed the footage, and edited the final video. It was my first time taking an idea from planning to the finished product, and the project introduced me to the world of professional video production.

After the great experience with GuiBike, I applied the same approach to InBurger, this time with a focus on creating content that showcased their burgers and encouraged customers to buy. I planned the concept, filmed the footage, and edited the final video with the goal of turning the product into the main focus of the content.

Bello was a challenging project because the ice cream shop was opening during the off-season. The goal was not only to attract customers, but also to establish the brand in the city and build recognition around its exclusive products. I focused on creating content that introduced the brand to the local audience while making its products the main attraction.

Espaço Conecta was a project similar to Bello in its focus on building a brand and connecting with a local audience, but in a completely different industry and with a much more selective target audience. It was also my first experience working across multiple areas of content production, including video editing, script development and prompting, graphic design, and the production of both A-roll and B-roll footage. This project helped me expand my skills beyond editing and develop a more complete understanding of the creative process behind producing content for a brand.

Marcus was a real estate agent looking to expand his reach across the Vale do Paraíba region. The project focused on creating content to showcase his property listings and strengthen his presence beyond his local market, helping his ads reach a broader audience across the region.

Giovana followed a similar approach to Espaço Conecta, but with a more specific target audience: parents of children and teenagers between 8 and 18 years old. Her work focuses on guidance and helping young people explore their future professional careers. The project involved creating content tailored to parents, combining video editing, creative direction, and visual communication to communicate these ideas in a clear and engaging way.
